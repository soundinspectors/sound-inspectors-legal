# Privacy Policy for Sound Inspectors

**Last Updated: September 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Sound Inspectors collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you interact with the Sound Inspectors Access bot, the Sound Inspectors channel, Sound Inspectors VIP, and related membership and support services on Telegram.

By using these services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Sound Inspectors, we may collect and store information provided by Telegram or generated through your use of the service, including:

- Your Telegram user ID
- Your Telegram username, if available
- Your first name as provided by Telegram
- Trial eligibility, trial start and end dates, and trial usage status
- VIP membership status and membership expiration dates
- Telegram Stars payment information related to Sound Inspectors, including payment amount, currency, transaction identifiers, subscription expiration information, and recurring payment status
- Records showing whether and when you accepted the Sound Inspectors Terms
- The acquisition source associated with your first entry into the Sound Inspectors access flow, such as Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, referral, direct invitation, or a general Sound Inspectors link
- Funnel and onboarding information, such as when you entered the access flow and whether scheduled onboarding messages were sent
- Responses you voluntarily provide to membership or cancellation surveys
- Information you voluntarily provide when contacting Sound Inspectors support
- Technical and operational records necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service

We do not receive or store your bank card details or other payment-card credentials. Telegram Stars payments are processed through Telegram's payment infrastructure.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use the information described above to:

- Provide and manage access to Sound Inspectors and Sound Inspectors VIP
- Determine eligibility for the one-time free VIP trial
- Activate, renew, expire, or remove VIP access
- Verify and administer Telegram Stars payments and subscriptions
- Prevent repeated use or abuse of the one-time trial
- Record acceptance of the applicable Terms
- Provide onboarding and membership-related messages
- Provide customer and payment support
- Investigate payment, membership, or access issues
- Understand how users discover and interact with Sound Inspectors
- Analyze membership, trial, renewal, and cancellation patterns
- Collect voluntary feedback to improve the service
- Maintain the security, reliability, and operation of the bot and membership system

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

## 3. Telegram Stars and Payments

Payments for Sound Inspectors VIP are processed using Telegram Stars through Telegram's payment infrastructure.

Sound Inspectors may retain transaction-related information made available through Telegram, such as Telegram payment charge identifiers, payment amounts, subscription status, and subscription expiration information. This information may be used to verify payments, manage membership access, provide support, investigate payment issues, and process eligible refunds where applicable.

Sound Inspectors does not receive or store your bank card number or other payment-card credentials.

Your use of Telegram and Telegram Stars is also subject to Telegram's own terms, policies, and applicable platform rules.

## 4. Trials and Membership Records

Each Telegram account is eligible for one Sound Inspectors VIP free trial.

We may retain records relating to previous trial use so that leaving the service, rejoining, or restarting the bot does not reset trial eligibility.

We may also retain membership and payment history as reasonably necessary to administer access, resolve disputes, provide support, maintain accurate records, and protect the service from abuse.

## 5. Analytics and Acquisition Information

Sound Inspectors may record how a user first entered the Sound Inspectors access flow, for example through an Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, referral, direct invitation, or general Sound Inspectors link.

We use this information to understand the effectiveness of our acquisition channels and the operation of the membership funnel.

We may also use membership events and aggregated statistics to understand trial usage, conversions, renewals, membership endings, and other service performance indicators.

## 6. Surveys and Voluntary Information

Sound Inspectors may occasionally ask users for feedback, including reasons for not continuing after a trial or reasons for ending a paid membership.

Participation is voluntary.

If you provide a survey response or contact support, the information you submit may be stored and used to understand your feedback, respond to your request, resolve issues, and improve the service.

Please do not send passwords, Telegram verification codes, two-step verification passwords, recovery codes, payment-card credentials, or other highly sensitive authentication information to Sound Inspectors support.

## 7. Service Providers and Infrastructure

Sound Inspectors operates through Telegram and uses third-party infrastructure and hosting services to operate the bot and store service data.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Sound Inspectors currently uses Railway infrastructure for hosting and data storage associated with the bot.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, manage memberships and payments, maintain trial eligibility records, provide support, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, maintain security and operational records, and comply with applicable obligations.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose.

Where information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, it may be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable technical, legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution requirements.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and operational measures designed to protect information associated with Sound Inspectors.

However, no online service, database, hosting platform, or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.
